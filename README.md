# AI-Augmented Customer Shopping Trends Analysis

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end AI-assisted Business Intelligence workflow using **PostgreSQL, SQL, Python, Power BI, and Generative AI**. The objective is to analyze customer shopping behavior, identify purchasing trends, evaluate sales performance, and generate actionable business insights through an interactive dashboard.

The project combines traditional data analytics techniques with Generative AI tools to support business understanding, SQL development, Python analysis, dashboard design, and executive reporting.

---

# Business Problem

Retail organizations collect large volumes of customer transaction data but often struggle to convert that information into meaningful business insights.

This project answers important business questions such as:

- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which customer segments contribute the most sales?
- How do seasonal trends impact revenue?
- How effective are subscription programs?
- How frequently are discounts and promo codes used?
- Which customer behaviors influence purchasing patterns?

# Key Insights

- Total Revenue: **$233,081**
- Average Purchase Amount: **$59.76**
- Clothing generated the highest revenue.
- Male customers contributed the largest share of total sales.
- Revenue remained consistent across all seasons.
- 43% of customers used discounts or promo codes.
- Only 27% of customers were active subscribers.

---

# Business Recommendations

- Increase investment in Clothing and Accessories.
- Improve subscription conversion strategies.
- Optimize promotional campaigns.
- Personalize marketing based on customer behavior.
- Improve customer satisfaction through product feedback analysis.
- Use seasonal purchasing trends for inventory planning.
